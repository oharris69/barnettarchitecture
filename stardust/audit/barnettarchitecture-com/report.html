Executive summary

The biggest lever is conversion focus: the heaviest dimension at weight 20, it scores 25. No page among the 48 carries a call to action, Contact sits inside the About dropdown, and the 29 project pages are reachable only through menus (F-005, F-006).

The 10 P1 findings describe a site that does not say what it is: 0 of 48 pages have a meta description, the firm wordmark is the first H1 on all 48, the home page has 0 words of body text, and no Organization or LocalBusiness schema ties the practice to Madison (F-001–F-004).

Accessibility and performance compound it: 8 of 208 content images carry text alt, the active-nav magenta (4.03:1) and the Send button (2.85:1) fail AA, the contact form is unlabelled, and mobile Total Blocking Time runs 1861–1957 ms on every sampled page (F-007–F-010).

The material for a better site already exists: project narratives are readable without JavaScript (strict AI readability 84–99%) and the originals are large (median long edge 3264 px), but they are shown at 646×443 inside a fixed canvas.

Stated assumptions

Rendered verbatim from audit.json#assumptions.

  3. Core Web Vitals sampled on 5 representative pages (/, /about, /residential, /green-ribbon, /copy-of-iowa) rather than all 48; all pages share the same Wix runtime.
  4. Social icon links, footer year and placeholder alt values read from the captured HTML of 2026-11-17; _brand-extraction.json's '© 2015 … unlinked' is superseded.
  5. The brief's premise that project slideshows are 'mostly 1024px source images' was tested against Wix gallery data and not confirmed (16% <=1024px); the finding is framed on display size instead.
